Data deletion requests

To request deletion of data associated with IdoLED purchases or support contact, email thehuangchengyi@gmail.com with the subject IdoLED Data Deletion Request.

IdoLED does not require an account. Projects, imported media, recordings, and exports are stored locally on your device. You can delete local app data by deleting projects in the app, clearing app data in Android settings, or uninstalling IdoLED.
